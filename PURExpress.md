### Equipment:

- Plate Reader
- 384 well-plate

### Reagents:

- PURExpress Solution A
- PURExpress Solution B
- Molecular Beacon (MB)
- HBC620
- Nuclease Free H2O (NF H2O)
- DNA templates

### Procedure:

Refer to spreadsheet for specified volumes.

1) Obtain PURExpress solutions from -80$\degree$C and thaw on ice.
2) Obtain MB, HBC620, and DNA templates from -20$\degree$C and thaw at room temperature.
3) Start up plate reader, confirm set to 37$\degree$C, and choose appropriate protocol.
4) Add DNA templates and NF H2O to 200 $\micro$L PCR tubes as designed.
5) Prepare master mix:
	a) Label 1.7-2 mL tube 
	b) Pipette mix Solution A before adding to master mix tube.
	c) Pipette mix Solution B then pipette mix with Solution A in master mix tube.
	d) Add MB and HBC620 to master mix
	e) Cap master mix and quickly vortex
6) Add specified volume of master mix to each PCR tube.
7) Pipette mix PCR tubes.
8) Add 10 $\micro$L of PCR tube to wells in 384-well plate (do not go through second stop to avoid generating bubbles).
9) Check for bubbles and apply clear plastic cover sheet to the plate.
10) Use a plate centrifuge to move any bubbles to the top of the wells. 
11) Put plate in reader and run the protocol.
12) Let reaction run for at least 4 hours.
13) Click STOP (not pause) on the kinetic tracker popup in Gen5
14) Add 3 $\micro$L of 10 $\micro$M MB trigger RNA to each well.
	a) Pipette mix several times in each well.
15) Cover with clear sheet, put back in plate reader and press play-> continue reading same plate.
16) Let reaction run for at least 2 hrs (can be overnight)
17) Stop plate reader and export txt file.
